# Sources and public-domain reasoning

Standard used: every included item had to be public domain **in the United States** for both the
**composition** and the **specific edition, scan or typesetting**. Anything doubtful was left out.
(US rule: works published before 1931 are public domain as of 2026. Faithful scans of public-domain
sheet music add no new copyright.)

typesetters placed them in the **public domain** (each file's header says `license = "Public Domain"`).

double-stops reduced to the top note) are released under CC0.

## Considered and left out

* **Klosé, *25 Daily Exercises*.** The only scan available is a Carl Fischer edition dated 1943. IMSLP
  calls the 1943 claim "probably bogus", but that's not clear enough, so it's excluded.
* **Klosé *25 Études de mécanisme* (Leduc), Mayeur *Grand Method* (1896), and the Ferling Richault
  1845 edition.** These are public domain, but they couldn't be downloaded here (IMSLP and Gallica
  bot checks), so they aren't included. They'd be welcome additions.
* **Modern Ferling editions (Hofmeister 1961, UE 1983, Billaudot 1969) and Higgins' CC BY-NC
  arrangements.** Excluded as modern editions or non-commercial licenses.
* **Mutopia items under CC BY-SA** (for example C. P. E. Bach Wq 132). Excluded to keep the repo simple
  (PD/CC0 only).
* **Cavallini caprices.** Public domain, but the range doesn't fit the alto. Left out.

## Original material

Everything in `pdf/01`–`pdf/09` was written for this pack, generated by the code in `tools/`, and is
released under CC0 1.0. The chord progressions are original and use generic harmonic devices. No
melody, solo or composition by any other writer is quoted, transcribed or paraphrased.
